\begin{abstract}
A trained deep network is one opaque object. No standard method
says which training example produced which part of an output, so a
contribution cannot be priced; two trained networks cannot be added
into a better one; and new data means retraining the whole. This
report describes additive networks, named here Sprig fields, in
which every stage is a frozen
basis read out by one ridge regression, a regularized linear
least-squares solve, the same calculation as fitting a line through
points, in high dimension. Nothing is trained at use time, replay
is bit-identical, and every output is a literal sum whose terms can
be attributed. A network is grown from scratch one frozen stage at
a time, or converted from a pretrained model by replacing its parts
with fitted stages at serve time.

The construction is measured on four tracks. On signed-distance
fields, the standard continuous description of a shape, it beats a
small trained residual network on every real shape where that
network converges, at less than half its error, and sits a median
15 percent behind a converged DeepSDF-class network. On word-level
language at toy scale, a model grown stage by stage with no
pretrained teacher reaches 151.6 perplexity against a Kneser-Ney
floor of 215.1 on the same split, where perplexity is the
exponentiated average surprise on held-out text and lower is
better. On conversion, the feed-forward layers of Qwen3-4B become
frozen teacher-key stages: a layer's own gate and up directions
kept as keys, read out by one ridge solve, reproducing the layer
exactly on held-out rows ($R^2$ 1.0). A basis diagnosis shows why
this is the one basis that can fit them: the layer's function
varies along its 9,728 key directions, and bases that miss those
directions top out near a third of the explained variance. Key
attribution compresses the stages, and the staged feed-forward
layers of Qwen3-4B serve from int8 packs, 2.05~GiB for all
thirty-six layers. Attention becomes a retrieval carrier that keeps the
attention sink, a local band, and routed key clusters exact; four
such carriers score 17.36 perplexity against the teacher's 17.28
and pass the free-generation battery; the delivered full
conversion
replaces the attention of all thirty-six layers with wormhole
carriers and the feed-forward network of all thirty-six with
int8-pack stages, so no layer keeps a growing key-value cache:
the network's state is 0.19~GiB at any context, 4.6~GiB of
package and about 10~GiB resident including activations. It
scores 0.5391 against the teacher's 0.5938 on the same
256-question sample, with perplexity 18.56 against the teacher's
15.80 on the forty-thousand-token slice, and continuation
repetition of 0.04 to 0.85 against its registered bar of 0.21:
the remaining measured price of holding every attention state
constant, recorded as this form's limit. It serves at about 12
to 16 tokens per second from 1k to 16k context. And between two
trained networks, both left untouched: one closed-form read
carries $+0.179$~nats of a $0.597$-nat gap into a frozen receiver
($+0.106$~nats of it pairing-specific against a rank-flat
shuffled floor); a domain differential ships as a 40--77~MB patch
against a 2{,}944~MB donor, with a mode gate that holds
outside-domain change at zero; and the intended production
receiver is the project's own from-scratch seed.

No prior work converts a pretrained network into nonlinear
closed-form stages that keep exact attribution, deterministic
replay, growth by addition, and composition under a measured gate.
The nearest line replaces pruned transformer blocks with
least-squares linear maps for compression and claims none of
those properties; the transcoder line gives additive attribution
but trains its replacement and does not serve it. Every borrowed
part is cited, and where a neighbouring method does something
better, its adoption is planned and named. The feed-forward
conversion is measured across all thirty-six stages, and the
delivered conversion carries the attention of all thirty-six
layers as well, with its quality limits stated. The benefits also port back to unconverted,
conventionally trained networks: the same closed-form reads give
a trained network's patches measured per-layer contracts without
any conversion, an audit shadow that matches across devices
exactly; its readout can be refitted in closed form on its own
output stream at no loss; and least-squares targets inserted
into training itself deliver measured per-layer contracts at
parity-or-better held-out quality. The negative results that
decided the architecture are published with the positives.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% ==================================================================

This work feeds GEODE, a network of frozen models and small
deterministic programs whose contributions are priced by measured
use, composed on demand, and replayed bit for bit
\cite{whitepaper}. The network family measured here is named
Sprig fields: the field is the additive whole, and a sprig is a
cutting grafted into it from a trained network, so the name states
the method. In formal prose, a residual field network. The
conversion in this report is published as `Qwen3-4B-sprig`
(converted models append the scheme to the base name). Models
of the family are built from named, versioned blocks, and
each block's value is computed by an exact, replayable
construction rather than by an opaque training run. A trained deep
network does not fit that discipline. It is one object: no standard
method asks which part of the output a given training example
produced, so a contribution cannot be priced. Two trained networks
cannot be added into a better one, so composition is unavailable.
When new data arrives the whole network is retrained, so growth is
a global event rather than a local addition. This is the state of
the art as the report is written, not a claim about what deep
networks can never do.

The report measures one additive construction against that
background, on four tracks. The field track fits signed-distance
fields, the standard continuous description of a shape, as a global
carrier plus frozen libraries of local corrections. The language
track trains an additive language model from scratch, with no
pretrained teacher, at toy scale. The conversion track takes a
pretrained language model and replaces its internal parts with
fitted additive stages at serve time. The transfer track leaves
both networks trained and untouched: one closed-form read carries
part of one network's behavior across into another, with the
differential artifact and the mode gate that ships it measured.
The scales differ on
purpose. The field and language tracks establish the construction
on small instruments; the conversion track is the serious-scale
experiment, one pretrained model converted, with mixture-of-experts
conversion as the next step. The four tracks share one core,
given in Section 2: a frozen basis read out by one regularized
linear solve, and the track-specific realizations that keep it
small and fast in high dimension, the ellipsoid mixture in
Section 2.2 and the frozen teacher-key stage in Section 6.1.

The wager is that an additive model built from closed-form solves
and frozen parts can stay within a bounded factor of a trained
baseline on raw quality while keeping the four properties a network
economy needs: exact attribution, deterministic replay, growth by
addition, and composition under a measured gate. The report
measures that wager on fixed instruments, records which lever moved
which rung, and states the boundary of every result, including the
places where a property fails and the measured reason.

Two metrics recur. Perplexity is the exponentiated average negative
log probability a model assigns to held-out text; lower is better,
and a value of $k$ means the model is about as surprised as a die
with $k$ equally likely faces. The language track reports
\emph{word} perplexities on word-tokenized text, which are not
comparable to the subword perplexities of modern tokenized models;
the conversion track reports subword perplexities on one model,
Qwen3-4B. Near-surface error is the root mean squared error of a
field inside a band around the true surface. Every number is
attached to its instrument in the section that reports it.

\section{The shared construction core}
% ==================================================================

\subsection{One closed-form additive stage}

The common form of every stage in this report is a literal sum,
\begin{equation}
f(x) \;=\; \sum_{k=1}^{K} w_k\,\phi_k(x),
\label{eq:stage}
\end{equation}
where the $\phi_k$ are columns of a frozen basis and the weights
$w$ come from one ridge regression, a regularized linear
least-squares solve \cite{hoerl1970},
\begin{equation}
w \;=\; \bigl(\Phi^\top \Phi + \lambda I\bigr)^{-1} \Phi^\top y,
\label{eq:ridge}
\end{equation}
with the penalty $\lambda$ chosen by deterministic holdout
cross-validation: hold out a seeded slice, solve on the rest for
each candidate, keep the best. Random features combined with a
closed-form linear solve are the random-feature and
extreme-learning-machine recipe \cite{rahimi2007, cho2026}. Use
is a single evaluation: no optimizer runs at deployment, and the
same inputs with the same seed produce the same output bit for
bit. A stage is frozen before any later stage is fitted on its
residual, the part of the target it left unexplained, so growth is
addition, never re-solving. Every later section inherits this one
rule: one ridge over a frozen basis with a cross-validated
penalty.

\subsection{The kernel realization: ellipsoid mixtures}

A radial-basis stage stores a cloud of centers and evaluates a
radial sum over it. The cloud is a density estimate, and it cannot
be thinned as a cloud: deleting centers deletes density mass, and
the stage collapses even when the kernel width is recomputed on the
survivors. The working form is a formula instead. An ellipsoid is a
stretched sphere with a center, a spread along every direction, and
a Gaussian profile. The cloud is summarized as a mixture of $M$
ellipsoids: each gets a center by clustering and a diagonal spread
from its members, and the spread is inflated by the feature
dimension $D$,
\begin{equation}
\phi_m(x) \;=\; \exp\Bigl(-\sum_{d=1}^{D}
  \frac{(x_d - \mu_{m,d})^2}{2\,D\,\sigma_{m,d}^2}\Bigr),
\label{eq:ellipsoid}
\end{equation}
because in high dimension a diagonal Gaussian's own members sit
about $D/2$ in squared Mahalanobis distance from its center, and an
uninflated kernel underflows to zero at its own center. One
closed-form solve folds the mixture into the readout. Two fit
targets were measured, and they differ sharply. Matching the
cloud's kernel matrix at the stored points hits a U-shaped limit:
past roughly a thousand ellipsoids the atoms narrow into spikes
that reproduce the stored points perfectly and the space between
them badly. Fitting the readout directly on the stage's training
targets removes the U. The rule that transfers to every track:
fit the readout on targets, never re-project a kernel matrix.

The mixture carries a level-of-detail ladder for free. Every
mixture size from $M=64$ to $M=4096$ scores within 0.034 perplexity
of the full cloud when the readout is target-fit, so one stored
artifact serves a coarse cheap evaluation or a fine one at the same
quality, and the evaluation budget selects the level. Later
sections use this form wherever a radial kernel would otherwise
appear.

\subsection{The property set}

Four properties are measured separately from raw quality, because
they are what make a construction usable as a network mechanism.
Attribution asks whether the contribution of each part can be
measured and priced. Replay asks whether the artifact is
bit-identical on every run. Growth economy asks whether new data
can add parts without disturbing the accounting of old ones.
Composition asks whether two independently built artifacts add into
a better one. The outcome differs by regime and is reported where
each is measured. In short: attribution and replay hold everywhere
by construction; growth by a frozen stage holds and growth by
re-solving one growing code does not; un-gated summation of
accurate stages fails, and region-gated summation composes.

\section{Fields: the construction}
% ==================================================================

A signed-distance field stores, at every point, the distance to the
nearest surface point, signed inside or outside; the zero set is
the surface itself. The field form this track produces is
\begin{equation}
f(x) \;=\; g(x) \;+\; \sum_{k=1}^{K} w_k\,\phi_k(x)
  \;+\; \sum_{m=1}^{M} u_m\,\psi_m(x),
\label{eq:field}
\end{equation}
a global carrier $g$, a frozen symbol library read out by one
ridge, and a second, finer frozen library fitted to what the first
stage leaves behind, with the first stage frozen and never
re-solved. The weighted sum of localized bumps is Blinn's additive
implicit-surface construction \cite{blinn1982}, and the
coarse-then-fine structure is the two-level geometry of Pan and
Skala \cite{pan2011}.

\subsection{The carrier}

The signed distance to any compact shape grows linearly far away,
with slope one, and a sum of decaying bumps cannot represent that.
The carrier is the coarse global term: its center is the mean of
all training points and its radius is the least-squares fit over
the far-field points, giving $g(x) = \|x - c\| - R$. The residual
the library must fit is $r_i = y_i - g(x_i)$, bounded by the
shape's spread. The two-level structure descends from Carr et
al.~\cite{carr2001} and Ohtake et al.~\cite{ohtake2003}.

\subsection{The symbol library}

Each symbol is an affine block anchored at a surface point: a
windowed level bump and a windowed slope ramp in a normal
direction, the first-order Taylor model of a signed distance near a
surface, which is exactly a ramp. A symmetric bump can fit only the
level; the slope column is the difference between the affine block
and plain Gaussian bumps. Blocks are random and seeded: centers
drawn from the training surface band with probability proportional
to the residual magnitude, so capacity is allocated where the
carrier leaves the most error, the placement rule of boosting
\cite{friedman2001} and greedy dictionary pursuit
\cite{mallatzhang1993}, applied to a whole library solved in one
joint ridge rather than greedily. Random symbols beat a learned
library severalfold on the reference instrument, in line with the
random-feature construction \cite{rahimi2007} and against the
dictionary-learning baselines \cite{aharon2006, mairal2010}.

\subsection{The joint solve and the regularization}

The weights come from the single ridge of Equation~\ref{eq:ridge},
with the penalty cross-validated over a fixed grid. The penalty is
the single largest accuracy lever of the field track, and the
mechanism is the closed-form analog of grokking \cite{nanda2023}:
the random library is overparameterized and contains both the
memorizing and the generalizing solutions, and the cross-validated
penalty, the analog of weight decay, selects the generalizing one.
A fixed tiny penalty left a measured 1.3 times gap to the trained
net; cross-validating it closed the gap. The lesson: overparameterize
the library and regularize it, do not shrink it.

\subsection{The second stage and the weighted solve}

One global ridge leaves a structured near-surface residual that
sharper windows at a fixed count cannot fix, because narrow bumps
tile a surface worse than broad ones. Two properties of the solve,
not of the basis, close most of the gap. The first is a second
stage: a sharper frozen library fitted by a second ridge to the
first stage's residual. The second is a weighted solve: the
second-stage ridge weights training points inside the evaluation
band four times those outside, because the metric that matters is
near-surface error. Both stages keep the field a literal sum and
the use-time path a single evaluation.

\subsection{The register-conditioned variant}

The register bank is the closed-form analog of the coordination
registers of Darcet et al.~\cite{darcet2023}: the top-$k$
singular-value directions of the training near-surface cloud,
fitted in closed form and frozen. Every query reads back its
coordinates in that frame, and the correction basis is the affine
library modulated by the readout, solved by the same single ridge.
The modulation conditions each local patch on where it sits in the
global shape. The register-conditioned first stage beats the plain
first stage on 30 of 40 shape-and-budget rows of the real
instrument, and the register block is part of the recipe on that
count. A trained latent-conditioned readout over the frozen library
fits a shape from a previously unseen class by one linear solve,
within 1.3 times of per-shape fit-once on seven of eight held-out
classes at the few-shot budget.

\subsection{The trained baselines}

The accuracy bar is a deep residual network, twenty blocks of
Linear--Tanh--Linear with residual connections at width 96,
trained with Adam \cite{adam2015}, mean-squared-error loss, and
weight decay, on the same data \cite{he2016}. The stronger bar is a
converged DeepSDF-class network \cite{park2019}, a ReLU stack of
three hidden layers of width 512 trained per shape to a convergence
floor. The trained signed-distance line around that bar includes
periodic-activation coordinate networks \cite{sitzmann2020} and
meta-learned SDFs \cite{sitzmann2019meta}. A shape where a trained
network stays above its floor is
excluded from that network's comparison, never counted as a win for
the closed form.

\section{Fields: results}
% ==================================================================

\subsection{The instrument}

The primary instrument is a synthetic 12-ellipsoid cluster with the
exact Euclidean signed distance as ground truth, 6{,}000 training
and 4{,}000 held-out points. Three metrics are recorded:
near-surface RMSE inside the surface band, the primary metric;
zero-set agreement, the fraction of near-surface points whose sign
is correct; and full-field RMSE, dominated by the far field. The
real-data instrument is ModelNet10 and ModelNet40, described in
Section 4.6.

\subsection{The construction space and the equivalence result}

The construction families are the fixed-basis forms, the
adaptive or coordinated forms, the frozen-symbol-library forms,
and the trained baselines. Three readings from
the construction space matter. A pure bump sum over a zero coarse
field reaches 0.290 near-surface error but breaks the far field at
1.347 full-field RMSE, because bumps decay. The carrier cell fixes
the far field at 0.245 but trades near-surface accuracy. The
trained residual net dominates at 0.055 near and 0.987 zero-set.
The gap is tooling, not representation: a joint least-squares
solve over the whole basis, which is exactly what a linear readout
is, cut near-surface error from 0.290 to 0.174, and adding global
random Fourier features gave the best far field of any closed-form
construction at 0.164. The remaining roughly 3 times gap is the
value of learned basis functions, and neither adaptive primitive
families nor coordination registers closed it.

\subsection{The regularization lever}

Two levers completed the accuracy story. The fixed-penalty form,
a frozen random library of 178 affine symbols read out by one
joint ridge with a small fixed penalty, reaches 0.073
near-surface error, 1.32 times the trained net, verified across
four seeds. The joint-accurate stage adds the carrier and the
cross-validated penalty, with library centers importance-sampled
by residual magnitude, and reaches 0.0486 near and 0.052
full-field against the trained net's 0.0551 and 0.054.
This is the single-stage construction that beats the trained deep
residual net on both metrics with one deterministic ridge solve and
no training. The apparent 3 to 8 times wall was a hyperparameter.

\subsection{Synthetic transfer}

One shape family and one regime is not a result, so the
joint-accurate stage was re-measured across eight configurations:
shape families with 5, 12, and 20 primitives; an elongated family;
a sparse family; the small-data regime at 1{,}500 points; high
input noise; and partial observation, where surface samples come
from half the primitives and the model must infer the unseen half.
The stage beats the trained residual net on near-surface error in
all eight configurations, at ratios 0.73 to 0.91.

The construction was tested on 59 real meshes from two standard
benchmarks, using the true signed distance to each mesh in a

stage alone, the two-stage weighted recipe, the trained residual
net, and the DeepSDF-class network, the trained arms counted only
where they reach their convergence floor. The
residual net reaches the floor on 23 of 59 shapes; the DeepSDF

Within the tested scope of per-shape fitting on real meshes, the

network, inside 1.3 times on three quarters of the shapes. The cost
facts: the operative recipe fits one real shape in about three
seconds on one CPU core and holds about 82 kilobytes of
coefficients; a converged DeepSDF network takes seven seconds on a
GPU and 2.1 megabytes.

\subsection{The property set}

Each property was measured on the joint-accurate stage and re-measured
where it mattered on the two-stage recipe.

\textbf{Attribution and replay hold.} The field is a literal sum,
so attribution is exact by construction; the measurement is a
per-symbol leave-one-out diagnostic on a held-out near-surface
slice: 130 of 200 symbols have positive attribution, the largest
single contribution is about 0.00135 and the mean is
$1.3\times10^{-4}$, a diffuse, priceable structure. The payment
rule is the network's single Shapley rule \cite{shapley1953},
applied at the level of whole field codes, never at the level of
800 symbols inside a code, where an exact Shapley value is not
computable. Replay is bit-identical by construction and verified
for every closed-form construction.

\textbf{Growth economy does not hold for the accurate recipe.}
Growing the joint-accurate stage's library in data windows and re-solving the ridge
moves old symbol amplitudes by a mean of 2.9 to 10.7 times across
windows, and up to 1{,}664 times at the maximum, while held-out
error improves monotonically: the reorganization is the mechanism
of growth, not an artifact of the solver. The greedy regime has the
opposite behavior, old blocks frozen and never re-fit, at the
measured accuracy cost. The two-stage recipe grows by a frozen
stage and improves near-surface error on every one of 102
shape-and-budget rows.

\textbf{Composition by un-gated summation fails; region-gated
summation composes.} Two independent joint-accurate stacks summed un-gated are
five times worse than either half; a shared scene-level carrier
does not fix it, because each stack's broad random features
extrapolate large wrong offsets outside the region they were fitted
in. The fix is a region gate: each code is multiplied by a
window that is exactly one within the band of the surface samples
stored in the code and falls off beyond, so a code is active only
where it has evidence. On all 13 two-object scenes tested, the
gated scene covers both objects better than either code alone and
preserves each object within 1.3 times its single code on 12 of
13; the one loss is two thin shapes whose corrections leak under
every gate tested. The boundary of that result is measured: gated
composition stops preserving each object once the objects overlap,
once the scene has five codes, or once the gate softens past a
sharp window; codes whose bounding spheres are separated by at
least a quarter of their summed radii compose. No window computable
from the artifact alone does this, so a composable code carries the
near-surface points it was fitted on, and the data contract covers
them.

\section{Language from scratch}
% ==================================================================

The second track asks the training-time question: can the additive
construction be trained on language from scratch, with no
pretrained teacher, and still compete? The model is a sum of named
parts: an embedding table, a content-attention read, an output
head, and a growth policy that adds arms one at a time.

\subsection{The construction}

Words map to a fixed vocabulary of the twenty thousand most
frequent training words, each carrying a dense vector of
forty-eight numbers. The embeddings start from a count-based
construction, a pointwise mutual information matrix reduced by
singular-value decomposition and normalized, the classical
count-based line of distributional semantics \cite{levy2014}. The
rule throughout is structure first: where a closed-form
construction exists it is the initialization, and a small gradient
budget refines it; nothing starts from random weights.

The long-range read is content-addressed attention over a causal
window of two hundred words, fixed keys and fixed query,
\begin{equation}
    f_t \;=\; \sum_{s=t-W}^{t-1}
        \softmax\!\left(\frac{\cos(e_t, e_s)}{\tau}\right)\, e_{s+1},
\end{equation}
which reads the word that followed each relevant prefix position, a
pattern continuation. This is the fast-weight-programmer idea of
Schmidhuber \cite{schmidhuber1992}, with the cache and
nearest-neighbor language-model ancestry \cite{grave2017,
khandelwal2019}. Two amendments are measured. The keys and query
are fixed, so content selection needs no learned controller, and it
beats both a fixed four-word window and a decay reservoir. Recency
is measured as a null at fixed features, which separates this read
from positional-bias constructions such as linear-biased attention
\cite{press2021}.

The head turns features into a probability over the vocabulary.
The classical likelihood head, a linear softmax with a word
frequency term added as a bias \cite{bengio2003, mikolov2010},
initialized at the closed-form solution and refined by the gradient
budget, is the single largest lever of the track. It takes the
model from about five hundred word perplexity, the score of a
regression-to-embedding head on the same frozen features, to parity
with the classical floor on those same features. A language-model
readout must state a probability over words; regression to an
embedding is the wrong function class, and this instrument measured
that on identical features.

The growth policy is the center of the track. Each phase trains the
newest arm, meaning its own learned query projection, to its
validation minimum, freezes it, adds a fresh arm initialized on the
residual, and re-initializes the head closed-form on the enlarged
feature set. Growth stops when an added arm cannot lower the
held-out floor by a fixed margin of three tenths of a percent.
Adding capacity one unit at a time and freezing it is
cascade-correlation \cite{fahlman1990}; stopping on a validation
signal is Prechelt \cite{prechelt1998}; fitting to the residual of
a frozen ensemble is boosting \cite{friedman2001}; growing on a
frozen substrate has transformer ancestors \cite{growing2025}. The
amendments are packaging, each measurable: the unit is a structured
content-attention arm, not a hidden neuron; each phase starts from
an exactly re-initialized head; the trigger is the overfit signal
itself. Two final steps address order dependence. Span order is
randomized within each phase, and after growth everything is
unfrozen for a short joint fine-tune at half learning rate, so the
greedy path is an initialization rather than a permanent
commitment. The whole model holds about 4.8 million parameters and
trains in minutes on one GPU.

\section{The conversion track}
% ==================================================================

The third track takes a pretrained language model and replaces its
internal parts with fitted stages at serve time. The teacher is
Qwen3-4B, an open-weights model of thirty-six layers, evaluated on
two hundred thousand held-out subword tokens, where it scores 17.28
perplexity. The design goal is a fully converted network, all
thirty-six layers: the feed-forward stages of every layer, from
the first to the thirty-sixth, and the retrieval wormholes of
Section 6.4 for the attention side. The delivered network composes
both. The
\textcolor{orange}{\textbf{[highlight convention: passages in
orange were measured on the pre-wormhole serving system,
feed-forward stages with native attention everywhere; they are
re-measured on the final methodology, staged feed-forward
networks with retrieval-wormhole attention.]}} The
frontier moved through one diagnosis, the stage's basis: the
layer's function varies along its own 9,728 key directions, and
the frozen teacher-key stage of Section 6.1 is the one basis that
fits it exactly. The compounding that once blocked chained
conversion disappears with the exact stage; what remains is the
compression trade of Section 6.2 and the serve-refit of
Section 6.3.

A transformer layer has two parts. The feed-forward network is
pointwise: each position is a function of its own vector alone, and
it holds about three quarters of the layer's parameters. Attention
is contextual: each position reads the others. The two parts are
converted by different mechanisms and reported separately.

\subsection{The stage and the basis diagnosis}

A closed-form stage replaces the feed-forward network directly. The
stage keeps the layer's own gate and up directions as frozen keys:
key $i$ contributes $h_i = \mathrm{SiLU}(g_i \cdot x)(u_i \cdot
x)$ over the $9{,}728$ key rows, and one ridge solve reads the
stage out over $[h \mid x \mid 1]$. Fit on forty thousand
teacher-stream rows, the stage reproduces the dumped layer on
twenty thousand held-out rows at $R^2$ 1.0.

Why this basis and no other is a measured answer, not a choice.
One diagnostic fits layer 32 with four bases under one protocol.
The layer's own directions reproduce it exactly, 1.0. Linear
scores 0.32 held-out. A radial basis of eight thousand sample-point
bumps scores 0.43, and sixteen times the width collapses it back
to linear. Eight thousand random directional SiLU features fit to
0.70 but generalize to 0.21, below linear: directionality alone
overfits. The function lives on the teacher's specific key
directions, and every basis that omits them leaves most of the
variance unexplained. The frozen-key stage is the one basis whose
error is zero, and its readout is refittable in closed form
wherever the served stream differs from the fit stream.

\oldsystem{Replacing the top layer's attention with a pointwise stage costs
$+0.18$, so the difficulty of predicting attention at the state
level does not translate into serving cost at the top. A dictionary
read replaces attention with a fixed projection to 128 keys and a
softmax read over the last 200 states. It scores 15.38
teacher-forced, below the teacher's own 17.28, while keeping a 100
kilobyte working set in place of a growing key-value cache;
removing the top attention entirely scores 15.49.} The reading is
that the top layer's attention is calibrated for long context, and
under the short-context convention of this instrument it is net
harmful for next-word probability, so any replacement that keeps
the pointwise part wins on that metric. Perplexity is therefore
not the gate. \oldsystem{Under free generation the converted forms stay
coherent for five hundred sampled tokens and their hidden states
stay inside the teacher's manifold band.} That closed-loop check is
the gate every serving form must pass, because a teacher-forced
win can coexist with a collapse under self-feedback; the decode
battery of Section 6.4 is its serving form.

Context length decides where the wall sits. Over spans of four
thousand tokens the teacher improves from 17.63 to 11.15
perplexity, so attention does real work in the long regime. \oldsystem{The
dictionary read still beats the teacher at every length when it
replaces one top layer, because long-range information reaches it
through the real attention of the layers below. When the swap spans
two layers at once, the replacements stop winning.} That is the
serve-distribution lesson: every stage in a chain must be refit on
the states its upstream replacements actually produce.

\subsection{Stage compression: key attribution}

The full stage stores the layer's own three matrices and costs
about the same as the network it replaces; its value is the
properties, not size. Compression is key dropping. Ranked by
contribution, mean key magnitude times readout norm, the readout
is refit at each kept-key count; the held-out $R^2$ ladder is 0.92
at three quarters of the keys, 0.81 at half, 0.64 at a quarter,
and 0.52 at an eighth, all above the linear floor of 0.32. Random
dropping underperforms the ranked ladder by 0.08 to 0.19 at equal
compute, so the ranking is what makes the ladder steep. The
stages are heterogeneous by depth: deep layers hold 0.95 at a
quarter of the keys where shallow layers hold 0.64, so a
depth-adaptive budget, more keys shallow and fewer deep,
dominates any uniform budget at equal cost. Every rung is a
sealed pack: the kept key rows, the selection, and the refit
readout, servable as-is.

\subsection{The chain, the serve-refit, and the battery}

All thirty-six staged layers serve at once. Each stage is fit on
the teacher stream at its layer, and the layers below alter the
stream the later stages read. The shift is measured layer by layer
by scoring each stage's readout on the stream the fully staged
network actually produces. \oldsystem{The shift is real and small: the
readouts hold $R^2$ 0.88 to 0.96 on the served stream, and a
serve-refit, one ridge per layer on the served stream with the
keys frozen, moves every layer up, the deepest layers by 0.027.}
Two stabilizers belong to the refit as measured, not as choices.
\oldsystem{The ridge is trace-relative at $10^{-4}$: at a weaker ridge the
refit overfits regions the capture rarely visits, regions
invisible to held-out $R^2$, and the overfit appears under free
generation as a decode-collapse loop on one continuation prompt.
Shrinking the refit toward the teacher-stream readout by 0.2
removes the collapse and improves every layer's held-out $R^2$
over the unshrunk refit, so the prior is kept.} Refitting is the
registered maintenance image: the stage is one solve away from
its serving distribution at all times.

The conversion algorithm closes with a fidelity repair pass:
after all layers convert, \oldsystem{every layer below the parity band,
held-out $R^2$ 0.90, is escalated one rung up the key ladder and
the battery re-runs. The band is the measured one: the battery
held parity wherever layers sat at or above 0.90. Key spending

one question of 256, indistinguishable from noise, so the budget
stays where the band says.}

\subsection{The attention chain: retrieval wormholes}

Attention is not replaced pointwise. Its softmax weight over past
positions is, in this model, concentrated in three places: the
first position, the attention sink \cite{xiao2023}, which carries
59 percent of the mass at the measured layer; a local band around
the query; and a few far positions that content routing can find.
The replacement keeps all three exact and condenses the rest into
one term per query. This report calls it a retrieval wormhole; the
name is an analogy for the far links, and the mechanism is as
follows. Keys are clustered with centers seeded farthest-first
\cite{arthur2007}, and each query routes to its top-8 nearest
clusters, hard routing in the manner of clustered attention
\cite{vyas2020clustered}. Each head then reads the sink, the band,
and the members of its routed clusters with exact softmax weights.
The mass outside those sets becomes a monopole at per-token share,
the far-field accounting of Barnes and Hut \cite{barnes1986}. A
census pass over the layer's real inputs measures each head's mass
split and marks a head dense where the mass it would drop, the
share that is neither sink, local, nor routed, is at least five
percent. At most eight heads per layer stay dense and keep exact
attention; the census is where the known heterogeneity of attention
heads \cite{clark2019, voita2019} enters the construction. The
carrier is built from the layer's own weights and keys, routing is
recomputed per query, and nothing is trained.

One layer converts cleanly. The instrument first reproduces the
layer's true attention from its own modules at cosine above 0.99;
against that reference the carrier scores cosine 0.994 with 0.088
relative error on captured windows. The swapped layer scores 15.78
teacher-forced perplexity against the teacher's 15.80 on a
forty-thousand-token slice, a delta of $-0.025$ against a $+0.02$
admission bar.

The four-layer chain sits at layers 25 through 28, with the real
feed-forward networks kept and attention carried by the wormholes.
It scores 15.85 against the teacher's 15.80 on the forty-thousand-
token slice and 17.36 against 17.28 on the full evaluation. The
stream error that staged feed-forward chains compound is absent
when the feed-forward side stays real: the served hidden state at
layer 32, the first real layer above the chain, reproduces the
teacher's at an aggregate $R^2$ of 0.9995. An oracle confirms it.
Blending the served stream toward the teacher's moves perplexity
monotonically from 17.36 to 17.28, so the whole remaining window at
the chain is 0.07 perplexity. The per-layer dense sets, re-derived
on the stream the swapped layers actually produce, match the
teacher-stream census at this chain length.

Carriers at all fifteen body layers, 17 through 31, with real
feed-forward networks everywhere, score 17.54 perplexity on the

layers, and
the refresh changes the dense set at five layers and recovers about

\subsection{What the transparency buys}

Two further capabilities follow from the construction. Both are
stated here as predictions with their instruments named; neither is
yet measured. The enabling mechanism is the distance field inside
the additive form. Every stage is a literal sum over a kernel that
is a distance field on its input space, and every stage partitions
that space into regions by nearest-center assignment, the same
geometry that routes the wormhole carrier. The network therefore
carries a geometry of its own knowledge: each piece of learned
behavior has an address, the region of input space where it lives,
with a distance from every query. The addresses say where a
foreign network's knowledge can be attached, and the residual over
the same geometry says where the current network is weak.

The first capability is knowledge merging across networks. A stage
is defined by its geometry and its readout weights and nothing
else. A stage fitted from another network's hidden states carries
that network's geometry with it, so it can be added to, or
substituted into, the existing network under the same gates once
the two networks' code spaces are mapped. The networks need not be
siblings: the map between any two code spaces is itself fitted from
paired activations. For sibling networks the map is the
permutation-matching line \cite{entezari2021}, with re-basin
solutions \cite{ainsworth2023} and optimal-transport fusion
\cite{singh2020}; where the map is bridged by a trained stitch
layer between frozen networks, or by relative representations that
replace absolute states with similarities to a shared anchor set,
it is certified usable before any weight moves
\cite{stitch2026,moschella2022,relgeom2024}. For arbitrary networks
the map is read through a lens-inversion diagnostic: multiple
images, one region of one
network appearing at several places in the other, and caustics,
near-singular Jacobians where the alignment flips under noise, both
measured on paired activations. A per-block correspondence census,
the wormhole census generalized to whole networks, locates which
blocks the current network does not cover. One network's knowledge
then enters another as a fitted stage, with neither network
retrained.

The second capability is data requisition directed by the network
itself. Attribution is exact and the residual is read over the
same region geometry, so the network's own residual map says where
it is weak: which regions carry the largest residual, which symbols
contribute least, and where disagreement with a teacher
concentrates. From that map the next data to acquire is chosen
before any is collected, the classical active-learning position
\cite{settles2009}: requisition examples at the weak regions and
the disagreement tail, fit a new frozen stage on them, and leave
every existing stage untouched. Growth becomes a measured
transaction in which the network names its gaps and the data fills
them. The instruments for both capabilities are the cross-network
merges, the block-correspondence census, the lens-inversion map,
and a real-scale growth cell whose student queries its teacher at
its own measured weaknesses.

\subsection{The similarity and the difference}

The converted network keeps the original's skeleton: the same
layer count, the same residuals, the same attention, the same
head. One stage replaces one layer, and a stage maps its input
vector to an output vector exactly as the layer did. A reader who
pictures a conventional network as a landscape, each layer a
surface over its input space, can keep that picture: the converted
network is the same landscape, re-tiled. What changed is not the
surface but what is known about it. The comparison below walks
each formula in turn.

\paragraph{The feed-forward stage and the layer it replaces.}
A gated feed-forward layer computes
$y = W_d\,\bigl(\mathrm{SiLU}(W_g x) \odot (W_u x)\bigr) + b$,
with the residual added outside. The stage computes, for the
layer's own rows $g_i$ and $u_i$,
$h_i = \mathrm{SiLU}(g_i \cdot x)(u_i \cdot x)$, and reads out
$y = W_r\,[h \mid x \mid 1]$. The nonlinear feature columns are
literally the same computation as the layer's; what the stage
replaces is the down-projection. Two columns are added, the
identity and the constant, so the residual path and the bias are
part of the fitted map instead of outside it, and the readout
$W_r$ is the one ridge of Equation~\ref{eq:ridge}. The visual
statement: the same hills, with their heights chosen by one
least-squares solve instead of stored as a dense pixel grid.
Compression then removes hills whose attribution is inert and
refits the heights, so the landscape keeps its shape at a
fraction of the columns.

\paragraph{The fitting rule and the training rule.}
The trained network reaches its weights by iterative gradient
flow over one global loss, with weight decay
\cite{adam2015}. The stage reaches its readout by the normal
equations of the same least-squares problem, one closed-form jump
to the optimum, with the penalty cross-validated
\cite{hoerl1970}. The two rules are the same optimum approached
by different routes: the ridge is the fixed point a linear
readout's stochastic descent approaches, and the penalty is the
closed-form analog of weight decay, the lever whose
overparameterized library selects the generalizing solution
\cite{nanda2023}. The difference is the target. Gradient flow
supervises every weight through the end loss; the stage is
supervised by its layer's recorded outputs, fit on the teacher
stream and refit on the served stream. One coupling disappears:
no gradient connects one stage's fitting to another's, so an
error stays in its stage, and a weak patch is refit without
touching its neighbors.

\paragraph{Attention and the retrieval wormhole.}
The dense row is
$a_{ij} = \mathrm{softmax}_j(q_i^{\top} k_j / \sqrt{d})$ and
$o_i = \sum_j a_{ij} v_j$, with rotary positions, head-normalized
queries and keys, a causal mask, and the output projection. The
carrier keeps all of it verbatim and rebuilds only the weight
row, per head. The census measures where each head's mass lives;
heads that are mostly sink, local, and routed are carried, and
the rest stay dense. A carried head scores its sink token, its
local band, and the members of its top eight routed clusters with
the exact softmax above, and summarizes the remaining mass as a
monopole at per-token share, the far-field accounting of Barnes
and Hut \cite{barnes1986}. Short blocks are dense throughout.
Two things are therefore true at once: on the kept keys the
computation is exact attention, not an approximation of it, and
the discarded mass is replaced by a mass summary, not by
per-key scores. Routing is geometric, derived from key
clustering \cite{arthur2007} in the clustered-attention form
\cite{vyas2020clustered}, where a trained network learns its
routing instead. The visual statement: the weight row is a spiky
landscape over positions; the wormhole keeps every spike, the
sink, the band, and the routed far keys, and fills the gaps
between the spikes with one smooth mass field per query. The
spikes are the wormholes, tunnels through cluster centroids to
the far keys that matter; the field between them is the
gap-filling term.

\paragraph{The norms, the positions, and the head.}
The root-mean-square norm with its learned gain, the rotary
position encodings, the embeddings, and the output head are taken
verbatim from the model. The conversion replaces the pointwise
feed-forward network of every layer and, in the attention-side
form, the weight rows of chosen heads; the residual structure,
the depth, and the head are untouched. This is why the landscape
picture holds: the network's geometry is preserved by
construction, and only the tiles change.

\paragraph{The expert router and the census.}
A mixture-of-experts layer routes through a learned softmax gate
over its experts. The conversion reads that gate directly; the
census pass measures which experts are live and how flat the
routing is before any stage is placed, and the expert bodies
convert by the same frozen-key stages as dense feed-forward
networks. The routing that the wormhole carrier adds, clusters
over keys, is the same hard top-$k$ shape as sparse gating, with
the rule derived from key geometry and measured by the census
rather than trained.

\paragraph{Where the properties come from.}
In the trained network, a layer's job exists only implicitly in
its weights. One global loss describes the whole network, and no
single layer's contribution can be read from it; finding a weak
layer means probing, attribution, or retraining experiments. In
the converted network, every stage carries an explicit contract:
it is fitted to reproduce its layer's recorded outputs on
recorded inputs, so the stage's error on held-out rows is
measured directly. Three properties follow, and sparsity is their
consequence rather than their cause. First, the closed-form,
teacher-forced fit removes the cross-stage gradient coupling, so
error stays local and refits stay local. Second, the residual is
read as error, not as size: a region whose residual values are
large but which the stage reproduces exactly needs no new data,
and only the difference against the recorded target, together
with how thinly the corpus covers the region, names a weak
patch. Third, every diagnostic quantity, the fit error, the
coverage, and the census mass splits, is attached to a named
region of a named stage, which turns ``the network is weak
somewhere'' into ``stage $j$ is weak in region $r$, and here is
the next data to acquire.''

\paragraph{The happy path and the not-happy path.}
These readings describe the happy path, a term borrowed from
programming for the most straightforward and expected scenario
in a process, the one in which everything goes as planned. The
name exists because there are always other paths and other
outcomes, and it helps a non-programming reader keep the two
apart: the happy path is the ideal situation, not the only
one. On the happy path the stage reproduces its recorded
target: the residual is then the layer's own unexplained
output, and reading it as error rather than as size is exact.
The not-happy path is where the
reproduction fails, a region in which the fitted readout does
not match the layer's recorded outputs even on held-out rows.
There the residual mixes the two things the happy path keeps
apart: the layer's genuine signal and the stage's failure to
represent it. Reading that residual as error charges the region
for a defect that belongs to the basis, not to the data. The
instruments keep the two paths apart because the fit error is
measured against the recorded target, not against output
magnitude: a region with a large residual and a small fit error
is on the happy path and needs no new data, while a region with
a large fit error is on the not-happy path, and the same
named-region reading reports it as a representational failure of
stage $j$ in region $r$, with the recourse it actually calls
for: a richer basis where the basis lacks the shape, more data
where the coverage is thin, and the original layer kept dense
where neither closes the gap. Coverage and novelty have their
own not-happy paths and are flagged in the same way: a thinly
covered region is reported as thin rather than folded into its
fit error, and an input far outside the corpus is reported as
extrapolation, so a residual computed there is labeled a guess
rather than an error. The not-happy path is therefore measured,
not hidden: the readings degrade by naming their own failure,
and because the fit remains closed-form and teacher-forced, a
failed stage is still isolated, its errors stay in its region,
and its neighbors keep their contracts while it is rebuilt.

\paragraph{The prediction: the instruments transfer back.}
The prediction is a registration, not a claim of novelty. It
was written before any of its three parts was measured, with
the instruments named, so that each part could pass or fail on
its own terms. Its ingredients are old, and each is cited where
it appears below: the probe line, deep supervision, dataset
cartography, self-distillation. What those lines do not
establish is the composition measured here: one closed-form,
no-optimizer instrument set attaching to an unconverted
network and carrying the maintenance laws of the converted
form. Each leg had a live failure mode, and each is stated
with its measured dose. Nothing guaranteed that a second
modality's blocks would reproduce at $R^2$ 1.0 or compress
along the measured ladder. A head refit on a small stream
measures worse before it measures better. A per-layer target
worth less than about one percent of the loss either changes
nothing or costs quality without cleaning a contract. The
well-known part is the ingredients; the measured part is the
composition and its boundaries.

The per-patch instruments do not require the conversion. A
conventionally trained network can be audited as it stands:
record each layer's inputs and outputs, fit a frozen-key
surrogate on them in closed form, and read error, coverage, and
novelty per patch against the original weights. Nothing in the
read alters the network. This is the audit shadow, and the first
part of the prediction is measured rather than predicted. The
shadow was run on the unconverted Qwen3-4B teacher, twenty
layers read on CPU and on GPU through one shared arithmetic, and
every reading matched across the two devices exactly.

Probing is old, and it is not claimed here. Fitting per-layer
readouts on a trained network's activations to watch its layers
is the linear-probe line of Alain and Bengio \cite{alain2016},
carried forward by the BERT probing tradition \cite{tenney2019}.
What the shadow adds is the instrument set and the discipline,
not the probe. The surrogate is a closed-form ridge readout with
no optimizer, and the three readings, fit error, coverage, and
novelty, are attached to named regions of a named layer, with
the same numbers reproduced exactly across devices.

The shadow transfers across modalities. On a trained vision
transformer, a six-block ViT scoring 77.3 percent on CIFAR-10,
the same frozen-key read is fitted from each MLP block's own
input stream, and every block reproduces at held-out $R^2$ 1.0.
Substituting all six blocks with their stages leaves test
accuracy exactly the network's own, 0.7732 against 0.7732. The
key-attribution ladder then compresses the substituted network:
0.7722 at three quarters of the keys, 0.7695 at half, 0.7519 at
a quarter, and 0.7235 at an eighth. The reading is that the
construction leans on nothing specific to language: its input is
a layer's recorded input and output, whatever the modality. The
vision MLP is in fact more compressible than the language
feed-forward network, whose ladder falls to 0.64 and 0.52 at
the same two points.

The serve-refit law is the second part, and it is measured on
the same audited network. The readout that turns hidden states
into word probabilities, the model's output head, is refit in
closed form on the stream the network actually produces when it
generates text on its own, one ridge solve with no gradient
steps. On the registered slice the refit moves perplexity from
14.759 to 14.726. On 2,300 stream tokens the same refit
measures 0.49 worse; the law is a convergence statement, and
the registered reading sits at 36,500 tokens, where the refit
never hurts and slightly helps: the readout can be brought to
its serving distribution at any time, at no measured loss.
Training a model
on its own outputs is self-distillation \cite{furlanello2018,
zhang2019byot}, adapting on its own stream is test-time
training \cite{sun2020ttt} and TENT \cite{wang2021tent}, and
retraining only the output map of a fixed network is adversarial
reprogramming \cite{elsayed2019}. The delta is the mechanism
and the claim: closed form, not gradient descent, and a
maintenance reading, not a quality gain.

The data-starvation sentence reads out on the same audit. The
residual map names regions, and the sentence separates the two
reasons a region can be weak: the data is thin, or the function
is unreadable. The test is mechanical. Refit a flagged region's
readout on growing training slices and watch the held-out score.
One region rose from 0.25 to 0.73 as rows grew from 2,000 to
40,000; it was data-limited. Another stayed below zero at every
size; it was genuinely unreadable. The two failure modes are
separated by measurement, not by judgment. Diagnosing data
through model behavior is dataset cartography
\cite{swayamdipta2020} and datamodels \cite{ilyas2022}. The
delta is the granularity and the instrument: the sentence is per
region, and the instrument is the closed-form surrogate's fit
error and coverage, not the trained network's training dynamics.

The third part reaches into training itself. Local least-squares
targets are inserted into a training run: at fixed intervals a
per-layer ridge target is fitted on a recent window of that
layer's activations, and the layer is pulled toward it. At a
weak target weight the term is a whisper and quality is
unchanged; in between, it costs a little held-out quality and
cleans no contract. When the weight reaches about one percent
of the loss, the measured result appears: held-out quality at
parity or better, and the per-layer contracts measurably
cleaner, a small effect on a word-level ladder. Giving
individual layers their
own objectives is deep supervision \cite{lee2015}, greedy
layerwise training \cite{belilovsky2019}, and local error
signals \cite{nokland2019}. The delta is the form of the
target: a closed-form ridge on a rolling window, no gradient
companion head, with the cleanliness of the resulting contracts
read by the same instrument that audits a converted network.

All three parts of the prediction are measured at their
registered scales, with the instruments named, and the
discipline is the same as everywhere else in this report. What
is not claimed: linear probing is not new, intermediate
training objectives are not new, and diagnosing data through a
model is not new; each line is cited above. What is claimed is
the measured composition: the closed-form instrument set, the
device-exact discipline, and the transfer-back from the
converted form to the trained form. The instruments attach to
any network; what requires the conversion is the frozen stage's
closed-form lifetime, not the measurement.

\section{The transfer track: behavior read from paired networks}
\label{sec:transfer}
% ==================================================================

The fourth track leaves both networks trained and untouched. The
conversion track turns a trained network into the additive form;
the transfer track asks how much of one trained network's behavior
can be read out of another through one closed-form solve. The
instrument keeps the discipline of the other tracks: frozen
states, one ridge solve, held-out rows only, a shuffled-control
floor, and bit-exact replay from the stored captures.

\subsection{The instrument}

Two networks that share a token vocabulary are run over the same
token stream: a receiver (Qwen3-4B, thirty-six layers) and a donor
(Qwen2.5-7B). At every position the receiver's head-input state
$h$ and its own next-token distribution $z_A$ are recorded, and
the donor's next-token distribution $z_B$ is read at the same
positions. The transfer target is the donor's distribution delta
in the receiver's class space, $d = \mathrm{clp}(z_B) -
\mathrm{clp}(z_A)$ with centered log-probabilities. The correction
is a rank-$r$ linear read of the receiver's own states: the delta
basis is computed on fit rows only, from the top $r$ directions of
the delta Gram matrix; the features are a 256-dimensional PCA of
the receiver's standardized head-input states, with fit-row
statistics only, plus a bias; one ridge solve at a relative
regularization of $10^{-6}$ times the Gram trace gives the
coefficients, and the correction is added to the receiver's
logits. Both networks stay frozen, there is no backpropagation
anywhere in the instrument, and the fit is one exact solve.

Two guards keep the reading honest. A shuffle-null refits the same
instrument with the fit coefficients permuted, so the capture a
parameter count alone can produce is measured, not assumed; this
is the control-task discipline of probe design
\cite{hewitt2019}. And the anchor CE values replay bit-exactly
from the stored captures, so every quoted number is reproducible
from the frozen artifacts.

\subsection{The validation}

The measurement stream is 8{,}192 token positions of a
Wikipedia-text corpus, 1{,}638 of them held out. The CE lines are:
receiver 3.1611, donor 2.5643, a gap of 0.597~nats; the rank-128
graft reads 2.9823 against a shuffled null of 3.0879, capturing
0.300 of the gap. In deltas: the real arm closes $+0.1788$~nats
of the gap (95\% CI $[0.132, 0.225]$), the shuffled null closes
$+0.0732$ $[0.040, 0.106]$, and the null is rank-flat --- the
calibration that a permuted fit still carries from the corpus-mean
delta, not a directional read. The pairing-specific part is
therefore $+0.106$~nats, and the worst corner of the real arm's
interval stays positive ($+0.027$).

The rank curve is monotone in the real arm and flat in the null:
$+0.111$, $+0.154$, $+0.179$, $+0.213$~nats at ranks 16, 64, 128,
256 (captures 0.186 to 0.357; 0.386 at rank 512), while the null
stays between $+0.073$ and $+0.078$ at every rank. The
pairing-specific excess therefore grows with rank, from $+0.033$
to $+0.140$~nats.

\subsection{What the transfer is}

The coherence profile reads what kind of object the correction is.
Agreement with the donor's greedy choice is 0.766; donor-only
top-1 choices fall below student-only choices at every rank, so
there is no argmax-level steering toward the donor; and text
generated under the graft carries no induced repetition --- the
graft repairs one the receiver already had. The correction is a
statistical recalibration of the receiver's distribution toward
the donor's, not a copy of the donor's decisions.

\subsection{Donor economy}

Two findings decide how donors should be spent. First, donor
quality is not transferability: a second donor, far larger and
stronger (CE line 2.3203 against the first donor's 2.5643),
captures less of its own gap, 0.173 to 0.223 at ranks 128 to 512
against the first donor's 0.300 to 0.386. Second, donor count is
not the lever: stacking the two donors, the second donor's
increment is 0.5, 1.3, and 0.36 percent of the gap at ranks 128,
256, and 512 --- not rank starvation, and the deltas of this
same-family pair are about 70 percent content-overlapping (at
rank 256, 0.659 and 0.725 of one delta's energy lies inside the
other's top subspace, at a mean principal-angle cosine of 0.787).
What is left of size on the table is donor dissimilarity; a
mid-stack feature block appended beside the head-input block adds
$+0.008$ to $+0.012$~capture. Splitting the fit by the receiver's
own state clusters and routing the correction to the matching
cluster raises capture from $0.300$ to $0.344$ at rank 128 (eight
clusters, nearest-centroid routing); a same-size shuffled-
assignment control reads $0.234$, so the grouping, not the extra
parameters, carries the gain. And one screen must precede any
negative reading: the donor must beat the receiver on the capture
domain by a stated margin before a fit is read (0.10~nats is the
margin used throughout this track), because a donor that is
already weaker than the receiver measures nothing --- the
donor-quality confound.

\subsection{The differential as an artifact}

The coding case makes the shape of the result concrete. A
coding-specialized 1.5B donor against the 4B receiver on local
Python source text: the donor's line reads 1.5257 against the
receiver's 1.7136 ($+0.188$~nats, the margin gate cleared), and
the closed-form read captures $+0.039$ and $+0.045$~nats at ranks
128 and 256 --- 23 to 27 percent of the domain gap. The artifact
that performs this is small: the rank-128 patch is 40~MB and the
rank-256 patch 77~MB in fp16, against the donor's 2{,}944~MB ---
1.4 to 2.6 percent of the donor's size --- and it is added to a
frozen receiver without changing a weight. Applied
unconditionally, however, the correction also moves text outside
the domain: wiki-text CE worsens by $+0.045$ and $+0.034$~nats at
the two ranks, against a pre-registered locality bar of a quarter
of the gain. The patch is a global recalibration with a domain
tilt, not a localized edit.

\subsection{The mode gate}

The resolution is a gate on the receiver's own states. A linear
discriminant fit on head-input states, fit rows only, separates
coding text from prose at measured accuracy 1.000. Multiplying
the correction by the gate keeps the full domain gain ---
retention $1.00\times$ --- and drives the outside-domain change
to zero (a float-epsilon residue). The shipped form is receiver
plus patch plus gate: tens of megabytes and a ten-kilobyte
classifier, with the class list, the input window, and the decode
contract unchanged, so the artifact composes like any other
closed-form capability. On a mixed stream --- eight alternations
of coding and wiki blocks --- the gate reads at accuracy 0.983
and the same conclusions hold: the full domain gain is retained
(and larger than on the pure stream, $+0.064$ to $+0.087$~nats at
ranks 128 and 256, because block context raises the baseline the
patch reads), the outside-domain change stays within
$0.006$~nats, and the hard and soft gates read identically to
four decimals --- the discriminant saturates on block-pure text,
so the soft form matters only where contexts mix token by token.
The transition band is measured: after a block switch the first
sixteen positions are ambiguous (mean gate $0.60$ on coding
against $0.48$ on wiki), and by sixteen to sixty-four positions
the reading is clean ($0.99$ against $0.03$). What remains open
is stated with it: behavior-level evaluation on coding
benchmarks rather than held-out cross-entropy, multi-donor
composition, and the cross-family case where the class lists
differ --- where the measured route is a string projection (the
donor's log-probabilities summed over the donor-tokenization of
each receiver token's character span; the audit reads mapped mass
1.0000 within eight donor tokens per receiver token, with 0.35
percent unmappable mass).

\subsection{The special case: the seed as receiver}

The instrument above reads one trained network through another;
the production configuration reverses the roles. The receiver is
the project's own seed: the from-scratch additive learner of the
language track, trained over the ontology corpus --- the wiki
slice with its concept vocabulary --- with the theme prompt
carried on the training segments, and admitted under the seed
requirements the growth line fixes: head utilization near the
participation ratio at the stated concept counts, a positive
singular margin at every reconditioning step, a measured
correspondence profile, growth by frozen stages, and a
from-scratch shadow student as the fallback for any domain the
seed cannot reach. Nothing in the patch interface changes when
the receiver is the seed: the correction still reads the
receiver's own states through one closed-form solve and is still
gated on those states, so the measured instrument --- the
validation, the rank curve, the size law of the differential,
the gate --- ports with the class list and input window the seed
defines. The prompt part of the recipe is measured at toy scale:
three organization pressures and the title prompt each move the
measured read-state geometry by at most the $+0.001$ inert band
against a $+0.01$ bar, so the prompt is a recipe choice whose
seed-scale re-measurement is open, and the seed-scale graft
itself is named in the closing list of open measurements.

Two read-interface additions are measured at seed scale with the
tables frozen. The tied read finds a planted fact --- its key
scores rank zero against the whole prefix --- but at unit
temperature the softmax dilutes the match with length (the
planted key's weight falls from $2.1\times10^{-2}$ at two
thousand tokens to $4.2\times10^{-3}$ at eight thousand):
widening the window alone does not carry facts; the score
sharpness is the recall dial. A carrier read over fixed centers,
reading the members of its routed clusters exactly, recovers the
planted facts at full-attention parity at eight thousand tokens
(recall 1.000 against 1.000 at matched sharpening; 826 visited
keys per query; 1.6~MiB of fixed state), while the same read
with only per-cluster statistics holds the measured contrast
(0.102) --- facts travel through the routed members, statistics
through the sums. Adding the sharper read column after training
is admitted by three gates: a zero-weight column is bit-exact
against the checkpoint ($117.21$), a closed-form fit onto the
model's own logits costs $+0.13$ perplexity, and a brief
head-only fine-tune improves the checkpoint by $1.0$ perplexity
($117.21$ to $116.21$).

What is not claimed here is a new mechanism: correspondence
between independently trained networks is established prior art,
certified by use (stitching, relative representations,
function-preserving transforms) \cite{stitch2026,moschella2022,
relgeom2024,fpt2024}. What is claimed, and measured at these
scales: the closed-form read itself, its guard discipline, the
differential artifact with its size law, and the gate that turns
the differential into a switchable capability.

% ==================================================================
\section{Prior art and position}
% ==================================================================

The construction borrows its parts from several lines of work,
named here with the difference stated for each. Two lines come
close enough to the core claim that the comparison is made
explicit, and the things they do better are adopted. The position
that results is at the end of the section.

The basis for replacing a layer with a kernel stage. A network
trained by gradient descent is approximately a kernel machine over
its training data \cite{domingos2020}, and a transformer's
feed-forward layer acts as a key-value memory over its inputs
\cite{geva2021}. Both say that a pointwise layer of a trained
network is the kind of function a kernel readout can reproduce.
The equivalence between the trained form and the kernel form is
itself prior art, in three strengths: frozen random features with
a linear readout approximate what trained networks compute
\cite{huang2006}, gradient descent on wide networks is kernel
ridge regression in the limit \cite{jacot2018}, and any
gradient-trained model is approximately a kernel machine
\cite{domingos2020}. What this report adds is the reverse
direction: an existing trained network converted, layer by layer,
into the closed-form stage, with the reproduction measured on
held-out rows rather than assumed, and the readout kept in
closed form.

The kernel and field line. Closed-form radial-basis surface
reconstruction is Carr et al.~\cite{carr2001}; multi-level
partition-of-unity refinement is Ohtake et al.~\cite{ohtake2003};
the two-level coarse-plus-fine variant is Pan and Skala
\cite{pan2011}. Random features with a trained linear readout are
Rahimi and Recht \cite{rahimi2007}, and the Fourier-feature
frequency analysis is Tancik et al.~\cite{tancik2020}. Recent
closed-form field methods include extreme-learning-machine implicit
representations \cite{cho2026}, Mat\'ern-kernel reconstruction
\cite{weiherer2025}, RBF-interpolated polynomials \cite{zhang2025},
and kernel-regression signed-distance fields \cite{dai2026};
ellipsoidal radial bases were measured by SE-RBFNet
\cite{lian2025}, which trains its shapes by gradient descent.
Stagewise residual fitting of random features is representation
boosting \cite{zozoulenko2025}. The difference is the target and
the discipline. That line reconstructs geometry from coordinates
and trains its adaptive shapes. This construction summarizes a
trained network's internal map or fits a field with one closed-form
solve, the readout is fit on targets and never re-projects a kernel
matrix, and the artifact carries a flat level-of-detail ladder and
the attribution property set. No found work converts a network
layer into an ellipsoid-mixture formula with a free detail ladder.

The language-model line. The content-attention framing is the
fast-weight-programmer line \cite{schmidhuber1992, ba2016,
schlag2021, irie2021, irie2022}, with the reservoir arm anchored
by echo state networks \cite{jaeger2001}. The memory-plus-local-
match framing is cache language models \cite{grave2017} and
nearest-neighbor language models \cite{khandelwal2019}.
Count-based embeddings are Levy and Goldberg \cite{levy2014}, the
likelihood head is the classical neural language model
\cite{bengio2003, mikolov2010}, and the classical floor is
Kneser-Ney smoothing \cite{kneser1995}. The growth policy is
cascade-correlation \cite{fahlman1990}, Prechelt \cite{prechelt1998},
boosting \cite{friedman2001}, and growing transformers
\cite{growing2025}. No found prior art combines
count-initialized embeddings, a fixed long-range content-attention
read, an overfit-triggered cascade of learned query arms, and a
closed-form-initialized likelihood head into one measured assembly
on word-level text.

The model-compression line. Removing layers and recovering quality
is ShortGPT \cite{shortgpt2024} and self-data distillation
\cite{selfdataddistill2025}; rewriting the matrices is SliceGPT
\cite{slicegpt2024}; replacing a band with one module is modular
transformers \cite{modulartransformers2023} and cross-architecture
layerwise distillation \cite{cald2025}; the activation statistics
such fits read are the massive-activation phenomenon
\cite{sun2024massive, shiexplain2026, ramanoutlier2025}. None of
these re-parameterizes the layer as a closed-form field. The
formula stage is not pruned, not distilled, and not made low-rank.
It is the same function written as a frozen-key readout, with the
key-attribution compression ladder and the attribution discipline
intact.

The replacement line, the nearest work on the conversion axis.
Four recent methods replace parts of a pretrained transformer with
maps fitted in closed form on calibration data, with no training.
ReplaceMe \cite{replaceme2025} replaces pruned blocks with one
linear map estimated by least squares and merges it into the
adjacent weights, keeping about 90 percent of benchmark
performance at 25 percent depth pruning. Ghosted Layers
\cite{ghostedlayers2026} derives the unconstrained closed-form
linear operator that aligns the boundary activation after pruning.
SHIFT-LLM \cite{shiftllm2026} inserts an affine residual adapter
at each pruning site, calibrated by least squares, to correct the
hidden-state distribution shift that downstream layers see, and
merges adapters exactly across consecutive sites. SubFit
\cite{subfit2026} works at the submodule level, choosing attention
and feed-forward submodules non-contiguously by measured
redundancy and giving each its own fitted residual bypass; at 25
percent sparsity it retains 84.6 percent of downstream accuracy at
2.42 times the perplexity, against 81.6 percent and 4.34 times for
the strongest baselines. The differences are three. Their
replacement is affine, and an affine map deletes a block's
nonlinearity rather than reproducing it; this report's stage is a
nonlinear key readout that reproduces the layer at $R^2$ 1.0 on
held-out rows, where the replacement line reports 2.4 to 4.3
times at a quarter of the depth removed. Their objective is
compression, and none of them claims or measures attribution,
replay, growth by addition, or composition. And their linear map
has no content routing: attention is deleted, not replaced. What
that line does better is adopted. SHIFT-LLM's closed-form affine
correction of the stream at a replacement site is the lever this
report has not yet tried against the residual stream error of the
compressed rungs, and it is registered in the open measurements.
SubFit's per-submodule redundancy census is the measured way to
choose conversion order and anchor placement.

The transcoder line, the nearest work on the attribution axis.
Transcoders \cite{dunefsky2024} replace a dense feed-forward layer
with a wider, sparsely activating one so that circuits can be read
through it; cross-layer transcoders \cite{ameisen2025} assemble
these into a replacement model whose attribution graphs trace a
prediction to features, and on vision transformers they turn the
final representation into an additive, layer-resolved construction
with faithful attribution while preserving zero-shot accuracy
\cite{cltvit2026}. The differences: transcoders are trained by
gradient descent with a sparsity penalty, they are a proxy for
interpretation rather than a served form, and replay, growth, and
composition are not claimed. What they do better is that their
attribution is measured by ablation, removing the top-scoring terms
and checking that performance falls, and their features are
readable by a person. This report's conversion track has so far
asserted attribution by construction and measured it only on
fields; the ablation-faithfulness test is adopted and registered.
Component-level Shapley values with efficient estimation are
Neuron Shapley \cite{ghorbani2020}; this report applies Shapley
at the level of whole codes.

The transfer-back line. Reading a trained network's layers with
fitted probes is Alain and Bengio \cite{alain2016} and the
probing tradition \cite{tenney2019}. Supervising hidden layers
with local objectives during training is deep supervision
\cite{lee2015}, greedy layerwise training \cite{belilovsky2019},
and local error signals \cite{nokland2019}. Diagnosing data
through model behavior is dataset cartography
\cite{swayamdipta2020} and datamodels \cite{ilyas2022}.
Refitting a model's readout on its own outputs is
self-distillation \cite{furlanello2018, zhang2019byot} and
test-time adaptation \cite{sun2020ttt, wang2021tent}, with
adversarial reprogramming \cite{elsayed2019} as the
retrain-only-the-output-map ancestor. None of these lines is
claimed as new; each is cited where its transfer-back passage
is measured in Section 6.6. The report's delta is the measured
composition: the surrogate is one closed-form ridge solve with
no optimizer anywhere in the audit, the readings attach to
named regions of named layers, and the transfer is measured at
the registered gates rather than asserted.

The attention-linearization line. Hedgehog \cite{hedgehog2024},
SUPRA \cite{supra2024}, and LoLCATs \cite{lolcats2024} convert a
pretrained model's softmax attention to linear attention by
training the replacement to match attention outputs and then
repairing with low-rank adaptation; LoLCATs does so with 0.4
percent of earlier methods' tokens, still billions, and the line
reports quality gaps and in-context shortfalls. The gain is a
fixed-size state and linear-time attention. This report's carrier
is training-free and keeps exact softmax on the retained keys, at
$+0.08$ perplexity for the chain; its cost is a gather-shaped read
whose wall-clock crossover is not yet measured. Hedgehog's finding
that spiky, monotone feature maps are what linear attention needs
to match softmax is the lesson carried into the diffuse-head
carrier candidate, fitted in closed form rather than trained.
Linear attention as a fixed-state recurrence is Katharopoulos
et~al.~\cite{katharopoulos2020}, with the fast-weight reading of
Schlag et~al.~\cite{schlag2021} cited above and the
random-feature instance of Peng et~al.~\cite{peng2021random}.
The carrier's far field is itself a linear-attention recurrence:
under a hard one-hot assignment of each key to its nearest frozen
cluster center, the per-cluster running value sums are exactly
the linear-attention state, so the wormhole is linear attention
with a data-derived frozen kernel, confined to the far field
only. Two things are not linear attention: the retained set --
the sink, the 512-row band, and the census-selected dense heads --
is exact softmax, and the two parts share one softmax
normalization, the far-field mass entering the same denominator
as the exact keys. Linear attention approximates the whole map
with a trained or random kernel; the carrier computes the near
field exactly and lets the recurrence cover only what the census
says can be summarized, with the exactness gate admitting the
replacement in the first place.
Kernel ridge regression as a token mixer trained from scratch is
Cubit \cite{cubit2026}.

The serving line. Heavy-hitter retention keeps the recent and the
highly attended tokens \cite{h2o2023}; paged attention with
central-memory offload is vLLM \cite{vllm2023}; hierarchical
key-value caching is LMCache \cite{lmcache2024}; offload scheduling
under memory pressure is FlexGen \cite{flexgen2023}; and
prompt-aware eviction is SnapKV \cite{snapkv2024}. The difference
is the predictor. Those systems decide which keys stay resident by
measured attention or learned salience. The dictionary read decides
with a fixed closed-form rule, the similarity between the query and
the stored keys, over a two-hundred-key buffer on the converted
network.

The compressed-memory line. Bounding the attention memory
without evicting is the compressive-memory family: Compressive
Transformers keep a trained auxiliary store of compressed old
activations \cite{rae2020}; Infini-attention adds a
linear-attention recurrence over the whole prefix beside a
local window, learned end to end \cite{munkhdalai2024};
Landmark Attention keeps a fixed set of landmark keys with
random-access retrieval \cite{mohtashami2023}; LM-Infinite
extends length through distance-shaped attention without
compressing memory \cite{han2023}; Dynamic Memory Compression
learns per-token allocation of the key-value budget
\cite{nawrot2024}; and the recurrent-architecture line holds a
fixed state over the whole sequence from training, Mamba and
RWKV \cite{gu2023mamba, peng2023rwkv}. The difference is the
rule and the replacement. Those families either drop tokens,
train the compression, or keep a learned companion module. The
incremental carrier is a fixed closed-form budget installed as
the attention replacement itself: frozen farthest-point cluster
centers from the first 256 rows, hard assignment, and running
per-cluster value sums that cover every token seen --
summarized, not windowed -- with the last 512 rows, the sink,
and the census-selected dense heads exact, and a bit-exact
dense guard below the first 256 tokens. Nothing is dropped; the
measured cost of the summarized far field is the repetition
limit, not a missing prefix. The width of the exact window is
this form's measured quality dial. Widening it from 64 to 128,
192, 256 and 512 rows reads 31.37, 25.29, 23.17, 21.12 and
18.56 perplexity on the delivered battery at fixed accounting.
A variant whose cluster centers track the served key
distribution, instead of staying frozen, moves the same guard
by 0.006. The exactness frontier is where the budget is spent,
and the summarized far field is where being wrong is cheap.

The sparse-attention line. Exact attention over a retrieved or
routed subset is Reformer's locality-sensitive hashing
\cite{kitaev2020}, Routing Transformers' content-based
routing \cite{roy2021}, and clustered attention, which routes
queries over k-means key clusters \cite{vyas2020clustered},
with centroid seeding in the k-means-plus-plus tradition
\cite{arthur2007}; the KV-sparsity serving family carries
the index forward: Quest's query-aware sparsity
\cite{quest2025}, RetrievalAttention's vector-index retrieval
\cite{retrievalattention2024}, MagicPIG's LSH sampling
\cite{magicpig2024}, and Squeezed Attention's budgeted
selection \cite{squeezed2025}, with the indexing machinery
itself the inverted-file and product-quantization line of
J\'egou et al.~\cite{jegou2011} and HNSW \cite{malkov2018}.
The low-rank forms are Linformer \cite{linformer2020}. The
local-window line keeps a span exact while abstracting the
rest: sliding windows \cite{beltagy2020} and window-plus-global
sparsity \cite{zaheer2020}. The
attention sink, the massive mass on the first token that the
measured layer shows at 59 percent of softmax mass, is
StreamingLLM \cite{xiao2023}; head heterogeneity, behind the
per-layer census that decides the dense dispatch, is the
head-analysis line \cite{clark2019, voita2019}, with per-head
variable context closest to Adaptive Attention Span
\cite{sukhbaatar2019}, and the outlier-direction structure is
the massive-activation line cited above. The monopole
approximation of the mass outside the exact sets is the
far-field accounting of Barnes and Hut \cite{barnes1986}. The
difference is the purpose and the gate. That line makes
attention cheaper for a model that is then trained or served as
is. Here the carrier replaces a pretrained layer's attention with
no training, is admitted only through an exactness gate against
the layer's own attention, a per-head mass census that decides
which heads stay dense, a swapped-layer perplexity gate, and a
free-generation decode battery, and it is measured as a chain,
where its value is that it does not compound. No sparsity budget
is chosen for its own sake and no span is learned.

The position. No prior work converts a pretrained network, layer
by layer, into nonlinear closed-form stages that keep exact
attribution, deterministic replay, growth by frozen addition, and
gated composition, and measures where the conversion holds and
where it fails. The nearest line on the conversion axis replaces
blocks with least-squares linear maps for compression and claims
none of the properties; the nearest line on the attribution axis
trains its replacement and does not serve it. The parts are
borrowed and cited; the conversion, the property set it carries,
and the measured map are new. The map has four parts. A frozen,
versioned, attributable construction beats a small trained net on
fields and passes the classical floor on word-level language at toy
scale. A pretrained model's feed-forward stages become frozen
teacher-key stages read out by one ridge solve, reproducing the
layer exactly on held-out rows, compressed by key attribution to
any point on a measured quality ladder. Its mid-model attention
becomes retrieval wormholes, with the sink, the local band, and
the routed clusters exact and the diffuse heads dense, and a chain
of them at the compounding span holds teacher parity on
perplexity and passes the decode battery. And three negative
results decided the architecture: basis mismatch, growth drift,
and un-gated composition, each with its measured cause. Two further
capabilities are stated as predictions because they are what the
property set is for: one additive network can absorb stages fitted
from another network's activations, and the network's own residual
map directs which data to requisition next. The submodule-level
closed-form merging of \cite{submodlin2025} and the byte-exact
replay protocol of \cite{kvgraft2026} are the nearest work on
those two axes and are cited where the predictions are measured.

The cross-network map. The nearest lines on relating two
networks' states establish that the map exists and certify it by
use: model stitching trains a light stitch layer between frozen
networks and finds heterogeneous foundation models reliably
stitchable when the stitch is trained by feature matching at the
target's penultimate layer \cite{stitch2026}; relative
representations make the map non-trainable, replacing absolute
states with similarities to a shared anchor set, with a
densification regularizer that clusters classes during fine-tuning
\cite{moschella2022,relgeom2024}; and function-preserving
transforms derive exact warm-start maps for architecture changes
between related networks \cite{fpt2024}. Each line certifies the
map by downstream use. None of them reads the map itself: a
per-layer correspondence census scored against a shuffled-control
floor, in the probe-selectivity tradition \cite{hewitt2019}, a
closed-form lens fitted from paired activations and scored on a
held-out grid, or an output-preserving reconditioning that
concentrates that correspondence under a warp-spectrum monitor
with an identity control. The superposition line supplies the
frame for the capacity side of the same measurements: computing
$m$ features in superposition needs $\Omega(m\log m)$ parameters,
while representing the same features can need as few as
$O(\log m)$ neurons \cite{superpos2024}, so an empirical capacity
line read from the states is a representing line, not a computing
bound.

A knowledge analogy: The trained form and the closed-form stage
are the same two strategies that manufacturing names subtractive
and additive. Gradient training is machining: start from a full
block of capacity and remove what does not fit, one global pass
at a time, so a changed requirement means carving a new block from
scratch and the removed mass is waste. The stage is printing:
deposit only the material the function needs, keys placed where
the layer's own weights concentrate and a readout fitted in one
solve, so a changed requirement is more material where the map is
thin, and every deposited part is named, attributable, and
re-runnable. The analogy is scoped to that one axis, how
knowledge is accumulated and revised; nothing beyond it is
claimed by it.

\section{Known limits}
% ==================================================================

The limits are stated as precisely as the capabilities.

1. The strongest trained baselines stay ahead on fields. A converged
   DeepSDF-class network is ahead of the two-stage recipe by about
   15 percent at the median, inside 1.3 times on three quarters of
   the shapes and with a per-shape tail to twice the error. Nothing
   here establishes parity with the strongest trained baselines;
   it establishes a bounded gap to one converged DeepSDF-class
   network. The levers that move the gap act on the solve, not the
   basis: a second frozen stage and a band-weighted solve.

2. The field result is per shape. Across-class amortization is
   tested in the closed-form sense only, and the DeepSDF-style
   trained-prior family's across-class strength is not reproduced
   or claimed.

3. The field accuracy results corroborate the random-feature and
   kernel literature rather than extend it. Every component of the
   field recipe is published, and the measured parity with a small
   trained net is what that literature predicts.

4. Composition is gated and scoped. Un-gated summation fails for
   the accurate recipe. Region-gated summation composes on
   separated two-object scenes and stops preserving each object
   once the objects overlap, once the scene has five codes, or once
   the gate softens. A composable code carries its training surface
   samples under the data contract.

5. The synthetic field reference is one shape family. Transfer
   across very different geometry families is not established by
   that instrument, and the real-data sign uses winding-consistent
   face normals because the meshes are not all watertight.

6. The language track is data-limited. Two million words is small,
   the growth policy terminated after three arms for lack of data,
   and the recipe has not been re-tested at larger scale or on
   subword tokens. The recipe is not pure closed-form end to end:
   the embeddings, query projections, and head are gradient-trained
   from a closed-form initialization.

7. Word perplexity is not comparable to subword perplexity, and
   every number in the language track is word-level on
   word-tokenized WikiText-2.

8. The conversion numbers are one teacher, one corpus, one
   evaluation convention, and the delivered network stages all
   thirty-six feed-forward layers and carrierizes all thirty-six
   attention layers. The delivered form is usable but not
   competitive on the binding read: 0.5391 against the teacher's
   0.5938, a 5.5-point deficit, with perplexity 18.56 against
   15.80, inside the track's registered coarse flag of $+5$, and
   continuation repetition above its registered bar. The measured
   price of holding every attention state constant is the central
   negative result of this track; the levers registered for it
   were a wider exact band, center tracking, more clusters and a
   re-anchored sink. The width lever is now measured and adopted
   (64 to 512 exact-recent rows: 31.37 to 18.56 perplexity at a
   fixed state size per row), center tracking measured inert
   (0.006), and the residual sits in the summarized far field,
   where the intermediate four-layer chain (17.24
   and 0.5547, inside the flag) bounds what more exactness can
   buy. At full keys
   with native-dtype stage compute and native
   attention the network matches the teacher exactly on the
   binding read, at native-scale compute. The stated deployment
   mitigation is tool routing, not in-head arithmetic: at launch
   the network should recognize and delegate mathematical
   computation to external primitives -- a computer or a
   programming language -- so that its job is to choose and call
   the right tool rather than to perform the arithmetic itself.
   That routed system is a new formulation, not equivalent to
   the converted network measured here, and this report claims
   nothing about it yet. The perplexity
   non-regression guard holds at the registered coarse bar, and
   the decode battery is a sampled stability check, not a proof
   that every prompt decodes well. Zero-shot accuracy is read on
   the converted network as its binding gate.

9. The spatial application of the ellipsoid representation, as an
   alternative to local convolutions, is an open direction rather
   than a result: the shift-invariance question that a global field
   raises against a local operator has not been measured, and this
   report claims nothing there.

10. Determinism is seed-level. The library, the cross-validation
    split, and the solve are seeded and bit-identical on the tested
    platform; this is verified, but it is a property of the tested
    numerical stack, not a proof for all platforms.

11. The wormhole chain is measured on one teacher, one corpus,
    and one context-length instrument: blocks of 512 tokens with
    a 64-token carry. Two continuation prompts diverge from the
    teacher on invented trivia. The 4096-token compute ratio is
    extrapolated from the measured 576-token block, and the
    compute is counted in multiply-adds, not wall-clock time; the
    retrieval is gather-shaped. The delivered full-carrier form's
    wall-clock ladder runs 12.3 / 16.1 / 15.6 / 11.9 tokens per
    second at 1k / 4k / 8k / 16k context single stream against the
    teacher's 35.1 / 34.0 / 27.0 at the first three points, with
    batching as the throughput lever: 126.4 tokens per second at
    eight sequences and 169.6 at sixteen, outputs identical to
    the single-stream path. The decode
    battery is a sampled stability check alongside teacher-forced
    perplexity, not a proof that every prompt decodes well, and it
    has run on the four-layer chain, the fifteen-layer body
    chain, and the delivered full-carrier form. The fifteen-layer body chain passes the battery and
    scores 0.5898 against the teacher's 0.5938 on the binding
    read, a one-question difference with no subject collapse.
    The full-carrier form fails the continuation repetition bar:
    its five prompts read 0.04 to 0.85 against the bar of 0.21,
    the far-field cost compounding across all thirty-six layers.
    The width lever is now measured and adopted (64 to 512
    exact-recent rows: 31.37 to 18.56 perplexity); center
    tracking measured inert; the residual sits in the summarized
    far field.

12. The closed-form field classifier is measured and bounded.
    The ellipsoid-mixture idea, fields over pixel coordinates
    evaluated instead of convolutions, was tested at matched
    parameters on CIFAR-10. Each class is a soft density mixture
    over position and color, one ridge readout per class. It
    scores 36.1 percent against a converged parameter-matched
    convnet's 56.4 percent, a gap of about 20 points at equal
    parameter count. The reading: matched parameters do not buy
    matched structure. The convnet's parameters are shared
    filters applied across positions, while the mixture's
    parameters sit in per-class ellipsoid centers and spreads,
    and none of the registered construction levers (a closed-form
    discriminative readout, patch-relative coordinates, patch
    descriptors) recovers the gap. The descriptor form is
    shift-robust, with no shifted-test drop, but capacity-bound
    at this scale. The vision deliverable is therefore the
    conversion track: a trained vision transformer's MLP blocks
    reproduce at held-out $R^2$ 1.0, substituting all six
    blocks leaves test accuracy exactly the network's own, and
    the key-attribution ladder compresses it along the measured
    curve of Section 6.6.

\section{Integration into GEODE}
% ==================================================================

The measurements decide what the additive construction contributes
to the network, and the implementation follows the measurements.
The first-class artifact is a code: a versioned, hashed object that
contains the carrier or basis, the frozen libraries or mixture
geometry, the ridge weights with their chosen penalties, and, for a
composable field, the near-surface points its gate reads.
Inference is deterministic: evaluate the basis, multiply by the
weights, add. One evaluation, no training, bit-identical replay,
verified across processes; the artifact's content hash is its
fingerprint. A code enters the model graph through the same
registration form as any learned representation \cite{whitepaper},
and the fitting implementation that produced every number in this
report is the one implementation the network runs.

Pricing follows the whitepaper's measured-use economy, and the
attribution measurements support it: per-symbol contributions are
measurable, stable, and diffuse, and the payment rule is the
network's single Shapley rule applied to whole codes, never to the
symbols inside a code. Composition follows the measurements: codes
compose under the region gate and by selection and readout
otherwise; the network never sums un-gated field codes at runtime.
Growth follows the measurements: a code is fit once at registration
and frozen, a new capability is a new registration, and within a
code growth is a frozen stage. The conversion stages enter as
serving artifacts under the same discipline, with the sampled
generation gate as their admission test.

The integration posture, in one sentence: the network registers the
measured constructions as codes, composes them only under the
region gate, grows only by frozen stages and new registrations, and
pays by the one Shapley rule at the code level.

\section{Conclusion}
% ==================================================================

One construction, four tracks, one discipline. A frozen basis read
out by one cross-validated ridge solve, realized as an ellipsoid
mixture with a level-of-detail ladder, beats a small trained
residual net on signed-distance fields on every real shape where
that net converges, passes the classical language-model floor from
scratch at toy scale, and converts a pretrained model's
feed-forward stages into frozen teacher-key stages read out by one
ridge solve, reproducing the layer exactly on held-out rows, with
mid-model attention replaced by retrieval wormholes that do not
compound and a converted network of Qwen3-4B, all
thirty-six feed-forward stages served from 2.05~GiB of int8
packs and the attention of all thirty-six layers carried with no
key-value cache anywhere, a fixed 0.19~GiB state at any context,
serving about 12 to 16 tokens per second from 1k to 16k context
and scoring 0.5391 against the teacher's 0.5938 zero-shot, with
its continuation and far-field limits measured and stated. And
with both networks left untouched, the same discipline reads one
network's behavior into another: a rank-$r$ closed-form read
closes $+0.179$ to $+0.213$~nats of a $0.597$-nat gap, $+0.106$
nats of it pairing-specific against a rank-flat shuffled floor,
adding $+0.044$ capture when the fit is routed by the receiver's
own clusters, and shipped as a 40--77~MB differential with a
gate that holds outside-domain behavior fixed.
Attribution and replay hold everywhere by construction.
The negative results are part of the design: growth by re-solving
drifts, un-gated summation extrapolates, and any basis that omits
the teacher's key directions leaves most of the variance
unexplained. No prior work converts a pretrained network
into a form that carries these properties; the nearest lines
replace blocks with linear maps for compression or train an
attribution proxy that is never served, and what they do better is
adopted. The map of where the conversion holds and where it fails
is measured. The invitation is to verify: every number is
reproducible from one deterministic implementation on a stated
instrument.

\section*{Open measurements and the predicted result}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Open measurements and the
predicted result}

The measurements below are planned and not yet run. The prediction
that follows them is written before the numbers so that the target
is fixed in advance. It is a prediction, not a result, and must not
be cited as one; measured claims replace it as each measurement
completes.

The open measurements are:
\begin{itemize}
\item the order-of-operations alternatives of the serve-refit:
  a second pass on the stream the refit itself produced, a
  layer-by-layer refit with recapture after each layer, refit
  before the fidelity escalation, and key selection re-derived
  on the served stream;
\item the denoising isolation: the full-keys staged network
  against the teacher on a large-$n$ battery, separating any
  smoothing gain of the ridge readout from the compression
  loss;
\item the four adoptions from the prior-art comparison: a
  closed-form affine correction of the served stream at each
  converted layer, in the manner of SHIFT-LLM and Ghosted Layers,
  measured against the compressed rungs' residual; a
  per-submodule redundancy census, in the manner of SubFit, to
  choose conversion order and anchor placement; an
  ablation-faithfulness test of per-key attribution on the
  converted stages, in the manner of the transcoder line; and a
  byte-exact replay protocol for the served body, hash equality of
  the logits across processes under a pinned configuration;
\item a census of the mixture-of-experts router on Qwen3-30B-A3B
  and one closed-form stage fitted against the router's top-8
  expert mixture, the open question of mixture-of-experts
  conversion;
\item cross-network merging: one arm supervised at the output by a
  stronger open teacher, one arm targeted at the hidden state, with
  the code-space map read through the lens-inversion diagnostic and
  the block-correspondence census locating the uncovered blocks;
\item the seed as the graft receiver: a from-scratch seed trained
  over the ontology corpus with prompt-conditioned segments,
  donor patches attached as gated capability modes, the
  transfer-track instruments (validation, size law, gate) rerun
  on the seed, and the prompt arm re-measured at seed scale (the
  toy-scale arms are measured inert for state organization);
\item theme-group concentration: an output-preserving
  reconditioning that trades immediate quality for tighter theme
  regions, judged at the composition level --- a temporary cost
  at the concentration step is admissible if the
  seed-with-patches system recovers it --- measured as
  seed-plus-concentration-plus-patches against
  seed-plus-patches at a matched budget, with the concentration
  step's own cost reported either way;
\item the ellipsoid-mixture port of the language track;
\item the serve-time residency gate of the consumer-grade target
  (partially measured: the composed form resides in 5.6~GiB and
  serves 16k context, and the seed's fixed-state carrier read now
  targets one million tokens with a 1.6~MiB state at the measured
  eight-thousand-token rung; the million-token residency and
  recall curves are the open part);
\item the from-scratch track at real scale, on a fixed 300--500 euro
  compute budget, GPT-2-class subword training in which the student
  queries a teacher only at its own measured disagreement tail,
  against an equal-budget random-query null arm. The student is
  preloaded with a count-built concept graph of the field, grounded
  node by node by the same queries, with the nested null that the
  graph must pay for itself. Wherever a question is checkable, a
  verifier, a Lean checker for mathematics or a simulator for
  physics, answers it instead of the teacher, with the gate that
  the verifier arm must beat the teacher arm at half the teacher
  budget. Where neither ground nor teacher can answer, an adversary
  over real observations, simulator output, and student creations
  grades plausibility and nothing more.
\end{itemize}
